# whyline-relay 0.2.22

`whyline-relay setup` can now create a plan by brainstorming it with
multiple models, not just the existing simple 2-agent draft. The role
wizard also asks for a backup chain, now that one shared chain exists
across the whole relay.

## What's new

- A third plan-source choice: "brainstorm" runs the existing multi-model
  brainstorm engine, then turns its final synthesis into a real,
  checkbox-formatted `plan.md`.
- The synthesis-to-plan step validates immediately and retries once on a
  parse error before asking you to look at it by hand.
- The existing approve/request-changes/discard gate for a drafted plan now
  covers both plan-creation paths, not just the simple draft.
- The role wizard asks for a backup chain (comma-separated, blank for
  none), wiring straight into the shared `[backup].chain` mechanism
  (0.2.21).
